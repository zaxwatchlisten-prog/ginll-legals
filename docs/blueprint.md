# **App Name**: PolicyZax

## Core Features:

- AI Clause Interpreter: A built-in AI tool that summarizes complex legal language into simple terms, helping users understand their rights in Section 3 and Section 4.
- Dynamic Legal Index: An interactive sidebar navigation that tracks reading progress through the 9 primary legal sections of the service terms.
- Subscription Billing Visualizer: A UI component detailing account tiers and usage-based rates as referenced in the economy section of the Terms.
- PDF Integrity Hub: One-click export feature allowing users to download a time-stamped, unalterable PDF of the zaxChat terms.
- Instant Resolution Gateway: Quick-action UI for direct connection to LBA Technologies Ltd support team as specified in Section 9 contact details.
- Contextual Tooltips: Interactive pop-ups for defining specific entities like 'Lba LLC' and 'Republic of Uganda Data Protection' protocols within the body text.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary Color: Deep Royal Blue (#4C4DFF), a reliable and precise tone conveying the security and structural integrity required for legal content.
- Background Color: Obsidian Night (#0D0D12), a dark, low-fatigue backdrop designed to enhance text clarity during extended reading sessions.
- Accent Color: Glacier Azure (#9EC9FF), a crisp analogous blue used for emphasizing highlights and indicating active document sections.
- Headline: 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif) for its high-tech, futuristic personality. Body: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for objective, high-readability delivery of dense legal clauses.
- Utilizes minimalist, hairline geometric icons that align with the technical branding of the zaxChat ecosystem.
- Single-column 'focused-reading' layout with optimized line widths of 65-75 characters for peak reading comprehension.
- Subtle, non-distracting vertical slide-up animations to denote transition between sections and provide user orientation.
